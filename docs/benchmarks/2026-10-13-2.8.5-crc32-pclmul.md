# 2.8.5 — CRC-32 PCLMULQDQ fold

**Date**: 2026-10-13 · **Machine**: x86_64 linux (Ryzen 7 5800H, PCLMULQDQ) · **Toolchain**: Cyrius 6.7.10 ·
**Prior doc**: [`2026-10-13-2.8.4-brotli-encoder.md`](2026-10-13-2.8.4-brotli-encoder.md)

2.8.5 folds CRC-32 with PCLMULQDQ on x86_64 ([ADR 0003](../adr/0003-crc32-pclmulqdq-fold.md)). Output
is bit-identical by construction and by test, so this file records only speed: two `cyrius bench`
runs of the 2.8.5 tree on one machine, back to back, one as shipped (`crc32 path: PCLMULQDQ fold`) and
one with the fold disabled at startup (`_crc32_hw = 0`, the slice-by-8 table that 2.8.4 shipped).
ns/op are machine-local; compare them within this file.

## SIZE: 47 rows, unchanged

The two runs' SIZE blocks are identical, and identical to 2.8.4's. A checksum cannot change a
compressed size; the gate shows nothing else moved either.

## CRC-32

| Row | slice-by-8 ns | PCLMULQDQ ns | Speed-up |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `crc32 1KB text` | 1,700 | 108 | 15.7× |
| `crc32 4KB text` | 6,117 | 315 | 19.4× |
| `crc32 256K text` | 357,771 | 16,657 | 21.5× (about 15.7 GB/s) |
| `crc32 256K text (slice-by-8)` (new row: the fold forced off in both runs) | 358,455 | 359,126 | 1.00× |

Crossover, measured directly on `_crc32_fold_table` against `_crc32_fold_hw` (200,000 calls each):

| Bytes | 64 | 96 | 128 | 192 | 256 | 512 | 1,024 | 4,096 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| slice-by-8 ns | 95 | 135 | 178 | 262 | 348 | 688 | 1,376 | 5,513 |
| fold ns | 44 | 45 | 46 | 49 | 52 | 68 | 100 | 287 |

The fold wins at its 64-byte minimum, so `_CRC32_CLMUL_MIN` is 64. Its cost below 256 bytes is mostly
the table finish (16 remainder bytes + the tail). On 4 MiB: 739 MB/s → 15,910 MB/s (21.5×).

## Where it shows in the codecs

| Row | slice-by-8 ns | PCLMULQDQ ns | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `gzip d text 4K` | 44,143 | 39,578 | 1.12× faster |
| `stream gzip L6 text 128K` | 2,408,360 | 2,283,531 | 1.05× faster |
| `gzip c text 4K` | 162,703 | 161,883 | noise |

gzip encode is dominated by DEFLATE's match finder, so the CRC share is small; gzip and ZIP decode of
compressible data see the most. Rows that never compute a CRC-32 (zlib's Adler-32, Brotli, xz, bzip2)
moved by ±2–4 % between the two runs in both directions: run-to-run noise.

## aarch64

The fold is x86_64 only. Under `qemu-aarch64` the aarch64 build keeps `_crc32_hw = 0` and computes the
zlib reference CRCs (1 MiB of zeros `0xa738ea1c`, a 100,003-byte pattern `0xcdb01cbf`) with slice-by-8,
and `fuzz_crc32` runs clean there.
