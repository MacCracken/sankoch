## Reading the numbers

- **Correct + competitive.** On ASCII source sankoch is within ~2.4% of `zstd -1`; on highly
  repetitive data the deep greedy parse *beats* `zstd -1` (63 vs 69 B); on incompressible data it
  stores with less overhead than the reference.
- **The wide-alphabet gap** (deflate.cyr +7%, CHANGELOG +17%) is the one structural item left: when
  a block's literals include a byte > 128 (UTF-8, binary), the direct Huffman weight table can't be
  used (header byte `127 + maxsym` overflows), so those literals store raw. **FSE-compressed weights
  (2.5.6, bite 4b)** closes most of this.
- **The ASCII gap** (~+2–11%) is parse quality (greedy vs zstd's lazy/optimal parse) and the absence
  of repeat-offset codes — the rest of 2.5.6 (bite 4c).

No wire-format SIZE gate (zstd output isn't bit-reproducible across encoder versions); this doc is
the informational ratio record per the "numbers don't lie" rule. Reference-`zstd -d` interop is
gated by `scripts/zstd-encode-smoke.sh`; round-trips by `tests/tcyr/zstd_compress.tcyr`.
