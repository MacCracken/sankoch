# 2.7.3 — zstd optimal / 2-pass parse (levels 7–9)

**Date**: 2026-07-21 · **Machine**: x86_64 linux · **Toolchain**: Cyrius 6.4.69 ·
**Reference**: zstd 1.5.7 · **Prior**: the 2.5.8 priced parse (the greedy+lazy default)

2.7.3 adds a **DP optimal parser** to the zstd encoder, gated behind `zstd_compress_level`
**7–9** so the default level 6 stays the fast greedy+lazy parse. The parser is a shortest-path
over positions inside each match series, pricing literals and matches under an evolving
statistics model with per-node recent-offset (repcode) state — mirroring zstd's
`ZSTD_compressBlock_opt_generic`. It writes the same sequence output as the greedy parse, so the
FSE/Huffman encoder is unchanged. The implementation is the DP probe built and measured during
the 2.5.8 investigation, now integrated, reference-validated, and made ratio-safe.

## Ratio-monotonicity (best-of)

The 2.5.8 probe was *worse than greedy on some fixtures* (very-regular record data). Shipping it
unconditionally at levels 7–9 would violate the `higher level ⇒ better ratio` contract. So at
levels 7–9 the encoder runs **both** parses per block and keeps the smaller (`_ze_try_seq_block`
best-of, with the losing parse's recent-offset advance rolled back). Levels 7–9 are therefore a
**strict improvement** over level 6 — never worse.

## Measured (best-of, level 9 vs level 6)

| fixture | L6 (greedy) | L9 (2.7.3 optimal best-of) | Δ | `zstd -19` |
|---------|------------:|---------------------------:|---|-----------:|
| source (290 KB Cyrius) | 74,437 | **70,785** | **−4.9 %** | 65,870 |
| records (420 KB CSV-like) | 16,770 | **16,627** | −0.9 % | 5,721 |

On real source the optimal parse wins (−4.9 %, closing part of the gap to `zstd -19`). On the
record fixture the greedy block usually wins per-block, so best-of lands just under level 6 — the
optimal parse never regresses. (sankoch's large remaining gap to `zstd -19` on records is a
*window / match-finding* limit — cross-record matches beyond the 128 KB window are unreachable —
not a parse limit; a separate future item, analogous to the 2.7.2 xz window growth.)

## Correctness

The optimal parse is a ~540-line DP with the known-hard repcode-state and price-model edge cases,
so it is validated hard against **reference `zstd -d`** (the spec bar — sankoch's own decoder is
lenient):

- **40 adversarial real files** across 5 distributions (random / low-entropy / periodic-record /
  near-constant / mixed-block) at level 9 → all decode byte-identical via `zstd -d`.
- **800 sankoch round-trips** at levels 7–9 across 5 strategies → 0 failures.
- Permanent coverage added: `fuzz/fuzz_zstd.fcyr` gained **500 optimal-parse (levels 7–9)
  round-trip tests**; `scripts/zstd-encode-smoke.sh` now reference-decodes **every fixture at
  both level 6 and level 9** (15/15, incl. the `hjsonrec`/`hasc` record fixtures).

The 43 gated wire-format SIZE lines are byte-identical (zstd encode is informational, not gated).
