## Context

What forces a decision? The reader wasn't in the room — set the stage. What problem were we trying to solve, what constraints applied, what was already true about the system?

## Decision

One-sentence headline. Then the scope:

- **In scope**: what this decision covers.
- **Out of scope**: what it does not cover.

If the decision has implementation specifics worth recording (signatures, on-wire shapes, file layouts), put them here.

## Consequences

What follows from this decision, both positive and negative.

- **Positive** — what becomes easier / safer / faster.
- **Negative** — what becomes harder / more constrained / slower; explicit costs we're accepting.
- **Neutral** — follow-ons that aren't clearly good or bad but matter for future readers.

## Alternatives considered

Paths not taken, and why each lost. Be specific — if a future reader is reconstructing this decision they'll want to know what was actually argued, not just the headline.

- **Alternative A** — short description. Why it lost.
- **Alternative B** — short description. Why it lost.

If an alternative could have won under different assumptions, name those assumptions explicitly so a future reader can evaluate whether the assumptions still hold.
