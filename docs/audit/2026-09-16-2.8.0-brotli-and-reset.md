# Sankoch — 2.8.0 review: the Brotli decoder and the per-profile arena-reset seam

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Scope:** everything new or changed since 2.7.15 (`da2a27e`):
- `src/brotli.cyr` and the generated `src/brotli_dict.cyr`;
- the per-module `_<module>_reset_tables` and the `src/reset_<profile>.cyr` dispatchers;
- `src/runtime.cyr`: the arena guard, the stranded-canary predicate, and the guard ahead of the AGNOS
  early return;
- the guards added to the unlocked public builders;
- `scripts/profile-link-gate.sh`, the CI/release wiring, and the Brotli tests, fuzz and smoke tooling.

**Method:** an adversarial multi-lens review fanned out over conformance, memory safety, runtime reset,
CI gates, conventions and regression/performance. Every finding was reproduced by execution (probes,
scratch mutants, guard-page harnesses) before it was recorded. Severities are the reviewer's.
**Toolchain:** Cyrius 6.6.4. **Reference:** brotli 1.2.0 CLI, a strict Python RFC 7932 decoder, and
google/brotli v1.2.0 sources.

---

## TL;DR

**0 HIGH · 4 MEDIUM · 15 LOW · 4 INFO. All fixed before the cut except R-22, which is outside this
repo (details below).**

No finding is a decoder memory-safety or conformance defect in the shipped code. The decoder held
up under every check:

- a 22,042-stream reference differential against `brotli -d` and the strict Python decoder:
  0 mismatches, same error classes;
- guard-page runs: 0 faults on unmutated code.

The findings cluster into three kinds of gap.

1. **Tests and gates that could not see the defect they claim to guard** (R-12, R-13, R-17, R-10, R-00/R-20):
   - `fuzz_brotli` and the tcyr never placed a buffer against a guard, so over-read and over-write
     mutants passed;
   - the link gate's AGNOS check was defeated by 2.8.0's own direct guard calls: the 2.7.10–2.7.15
     regression, reintroduced, passed 9 of 11 profiles;
   - the stranded-canary predicate was pinned in one direction only;
   - the NUL-literal rule was prose.

   Each is now proven by a mutant that fails.
2. **Load-dependent or hand-kept CI inputs** (R-18/R-21, R-06/R-19): a wall-clock IMTF budget that
   fails under contention or emulation, and profile lists repeated by hand in two workflows.
3. **Release hygiene** (R-02 to R-05, R-07 to R-09, R-14 to R-16): build-phase scaffolding, dead
   code, citations of an uncommitted design doc, a non-runnable extractor, and an invalid SPDX line.
   The one with a production edge was R-14: a plain global test hook that could turn any decode into
   an empty success. It is now a `header_only` parameter on an internal function.

## Verification of the fixes

- **R-12 / R-13.** Two scratch mutants were run against the fixed harnesses:
  - M1: `_brd_uncompressed` word loop runs to `i < mlen`;
  - M2: M1 plus a `_brd_copy` tail that stores whole words past `end`.

  Both SIGSEGV `fuzz_brotli` and `brotli_decompress.tcyr`. Before the fix both printed green. M1 needed
  the new `hello.q0.br` seed to reach an uncompressed meta-block in the fuzz.
- **R-17.**
  - Mutation: with `_sankoch_arena_guard()` moved into the `#ifndef CYRIUS_TARGET_AGNOS` branch and
    all 12 bundles regenerated, the gate fails **11 of 11** alloc-bearing profiles on the lock-only
    probe. `core` stays green: it has no runtime.
  - Against the committed 2.7.15 `dist/` (`git show da2a27e:dist/*`), every alloc-bearing profile
    fails. The 8 codec profiles fail registration, link and the lock-only AGNOS probe. The full
    bundle fails the lock-only probe with a dead guard. `brotli` and `woff` did not exist and report
    missing bundles.
- **R-10 / R-11.** Each new `arena_reset.tcyr` cell fails against its mutant (the dropped
  `_heap_base` check; the canary via the fault seam and without the lost flag) and passes on the
  release tree: 107/107.
- **R-21.** T12 reads 0.19 (decode / bytewise reference) on an idle host, against a 0.50 bound.

## Not done

- **The libbrotlidec 200k mutation differential was not run** for this release. Conformance evidence is
  the 22,042-stream reference differential, the 217-row committed corpus (`brotli-smoke.sh`, 1008/1008
  including google testdata, a q0–11 x w10–24 sweep, the LiberationSans WOFF2-shaped stream and aarch64
  under qemu), and `fuzz_brotli`.
- **R-22** belongs to the upstream cyrius issue files, which this release does not edit. Its corrected
  figure is already there, and the breakdown is recorded in [ADR 0001](../adr/0001-brotli-decoder-placement.md).
